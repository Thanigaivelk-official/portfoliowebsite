## Product Principles

1. **Proof Over Claims:** Feature real, functional architectures, verified live deployments, and readable GitHub repositories.
2. **Clear Technical Hierarchy:** Make core backend and full-stack capabilities immediately obvious to recruiters within the first 5 seconds.
3. **Restrained Craft:** Use 3D particles, glassmorphism, and subtle Framer Motion animations to elevate presentation without compromising page speed or accessibility.
4. **Honest Proficiency:** Group skills with clear qualitative tiers (Proficient, Familiar, Learning) to foster trust and technical credibility.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Responsive layout adapting seamlessly across mobile, tablet, and widescreen desktop monitors.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` for animations and interactive 3D particle canvas.
- Maintain high contrast ratios on dark backgrounds and ensure keyboard-navigable interactive controls.
